PrintCal: Print Your Calendar

Print the calendar already on your phone.

App Store · coming soon Google Play · coming soon

Planned for early December 2026, in time to print next year's calendar.

PrintCal is an iPhone, iPad and Android app that prints the calendars you already have (iCloud, Google, Outlook, Exchange and any other calendar on the phone) as clean, readable pages. Pick a month, a week or a list, then print with AirPrint or the Android print service, or save a PDF. The iPhone Calendar app and the Google Calendar app have no print button; PrintCal is that button.

Nothing leaves your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics, no internet access. PrintCal reads your calendars on the phone and never changes them. App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected. Google Play Data safety: no data collected or shared.

How to print your calendar from your phone

  1. Open PrintCal and allow calendar access. Access is read-only; the app never adds, changes or deletes an event.
  2. Your current month appears already laid out. Switch to Week or List if you prefer, and tick the calendars to include.
  3. Adjust Style if you like: text size, bold or Large Print, details, paper.
  4. Tap Print to use your phone's print dialog (AirPrint or the Android print service), or Share to save a PDF to Files, Google Drive or Downloads.

Good to know

  • The app is in English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese (Brazil) and Japanese. Month and weekday names on the page follow your phone's language; the page's own small labels (such as "All day") are in English in this release.
  • PrintCal prints the calendars your phone's Calendar app shows. A calendar switched off there is hidden from every app, including this one.
  • Reminders printing is available on iPhone and iPad only; Android has no Reminders.
