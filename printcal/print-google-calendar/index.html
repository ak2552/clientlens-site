PrintCal: Print Your Calendar

How to print Google Calendar from your phone

Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version The Google Calendar app on Android and iPhone has no print button. The Google Calendar website does, so you can print from a computer, or from your phone's browser in desktop mode. For a page made for paper, with every event and big text, an app on the phone such as PrintCal prints the same calendar directly.

Option 1: The Google Calendar website on a computer (built in, free)

  1. Go to calendar.google.com and sign in.
  2. Pick the view at the top right: Month, Week, Day or Schedule. Tick the calendars to include in the list on the left.
  3. Click the Settings gear and choose Print.
  4. Set the date range, font size, orientation, colour or black and white, and whether to show events you declined. The preview updates as you go.
  5. Click Print, then choose a printer or Save as PDF in the browser's print dialog.

Good for: anyone with a computer; it's free and it's Google's own layout. Watch for: a month page shows titles only, and a busy day shows "+N more" with the rest left off the page. To print event details, switch to Schedule view first: it prints each event with its time, one line after another, though without the grid. Larger font sizes make a month spill onto more pages.

Option 2: The same website, from your phone's browser

The website works on a phone too, if you ask for the desktop version.

  1. Open calendar.google.com in your browser (not the app). If it opens the app or a simple mobile page, go back to the browser tab.
  2. Switch to desktop mode. Chrome (Android and iPhone): the ⋮ menu → Desktop site. Safari (iPhone): the aA button in the address bar → Request Desktop Website.
  3. Follow the steps of Option 1: Settings gear → Print → set the options → Print. In the phone's print dialog, choose a printer or Save as PDF.

Good for: an emergency when no computer is near. Watch for: the desktop page is tiny on a phone and the Print dialog is fiddly; zoom in to reach the controls. The result is the same as Option 1.

Option 3: Print straight from the phone with PrintCal

PrintCal reads the calendars synced to your phone, including Google Calendar, and lays them out as a page made for paper: big text, every event, a legend for your calendars.

On Android

  1. Make sure your Google account syncs its calendar: Android Settings → Accounts (or Passwords & accounts) → your Google account → Calendar sync on. If the calendar is ticked in the Google Calendar app, it is available to PrintCal.
  2. Open PrintCal and Allow calendar access. Access is read-only; the app never changes an event and has no internet access.
  3. The current month appears. Tick the Google calendars you want in the Calendars sheet. Switch to Week or List if you prefer.
  4. In Style, choose text size, bold or Large Print, details (end times, location, notes) and the paper size.
  5. Tap Print to use Android's print service (most Wi-Fi printers), or Share to save a PDF to Google Drive or Downloads.

On iPhone or iPad

  1. Add your Google account to the iPhone's calendars if it isn't already: Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account → Google, with Calendars turned on. (On older iOS versions: Settings → Calendar → Accounts.) Your Google events then appear in the iPhone Calendar app.
  2. Open PrintCal and allow calendar access, choosing Full Access.
  3. Tick the Google calendars in the Calendars sheet, set Style, then Print with AirPrint or Share a PDF.

Good for: a fridge or door calendar you can read from across the room, a work week with exact times, a list with notes and locations, all from the phone. When a day is too full for the grid, the extra events continue on an overflow page instead of being left off. Watch for: PrintCal shows the calendars your phone's calendar app shows; a Google calendar unticked in Google Calendar, or an account with calendar sync off, is hidden from every app. Month, week and list are free; day and year pages are part of the one-time Pro purchase.

Good to know

  • Google Tasks are not part of a calendar's events, so none of these options prints them as calendar entries. Open Google Tasks itself for a list.
  • Holidays and birthdays come from their own calendars. Untick them before printing if they crowd the page; in PrintCal they have their own switches.
  • Letter or A4. Pick the same paper size in the print dialog that the page was made for, or the printer will shrink or crop it.
