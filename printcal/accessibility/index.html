PrintCal: Print Your Calendar

Accessibility

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Our goal Everyone should be able to print their calendar, and the printed page should be readable by the people it is for. We aim for the app to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA on iPhone and Android.

The app

  • VoiceOver and TalkBack. Buttons, toggles, checkboxes and choices are labelled and report their state, such as "checked" or "selected". Screen titles are announced as headings. On iPhone, error messages are read out by VoiceOver when they appear.
  • Larger text. The app's own text follows your phone's text size setting, and screens, including the Pro screen, scroll to fit. A few small labels, such as the toolbar and the page counter, may be shortened at the largest sizes.
  • Colour and contrast. Text and controls are designed to meet WCAG AA contrast; we will confirm it on devices before launch. In the app, nothing is shown by colour alone: a calendar's colour always has its name next to it.
  • Touch targets. Buttons and links are designed to be at least 44 × 44 points.
  • No animation. The preview has no animations; it simply redraws when you change a setting.
  • Any orientation. The app works in portrait and landscape, including on iPad.
  • No time limits and no flashing content.

The printed page

  • Large Print preset. One tap sets event text to 16 pt bold and day numbers to 22 pt, in black on white, using Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface designed for readers with low vision.
  • Text size and bold. Four sizes with a live preview, and auto-fit, which grows text to fill the space a light month leaves free.
  • Black-and-white printing. Calendars are told apart by outline, solid and hatched styles and by their initial, with a legend, so a mono printer or a reader who cannot tell colours apart loses nothing.
  • No event is dropped. When a day is full, events continue on an extra page (on by default). A title too long for its space ends with "…".

Known limits

  • Screen readers can't yet read the page preview itself; the events are read in your phone's calendar app. An accessible list of the events on the page is planned.
  • The app uses a light appearance only, even when the phone is set to dark mode. The paper in the preview is white, as it will print.
  • The page preview does not grow with your phone's text size, because it shows the page at its printed proportions. Pinch to zoom in on it, or change the page's own text size in Style.
  • The PDF is a printed layout. It is not a tagged PDF, so screen readers on a computer may not read it in order.
  • The page's own small labels, such as "All day" and "+N more", are in English in this release.

How we test

Before launch we will go through calendar access, the month, week and list pages, Style and Print with VoiceOver on iPhone and TalkBack on Android, at default and at the largest text size, and check colour contrast against WCAG 2.2. This page will list the date of each check once it has been done.

Tell us about a problem

If something in the app, on a printed page or on this website is hard to use with your setup, please write to us and say what you were trying to do and which phone and assistive technology you use. We aim to reply within 5 working days.
