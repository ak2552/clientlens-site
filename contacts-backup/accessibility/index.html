Contacts Backup, Export, Merge

Accessibility

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Our goal Everyone should be able to back up, restore, export and merge their contacts. We aim for the app to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA on iPhone and Android.

What the app supports

  • VoiceOver and TalkBack. Buttons, toggles, checkboxes and choices are labelled and report their state, such as "checked" or "selected". Screen titles are announced as headings.
  • Spoken progress. Backups, merges and restores announce their progress and results, so you don't need to watch the screen.
  • Larger text. Text follows your phone's text size setting. Lists and buttons grow to fit instead of cutting words off. Tab labels grow up to one and a half times.
  • Colour and contrast. Text and controls meet AA contrast. Information shown in colour, such as a field that will be added during a merge, is also given in words.
  • Touch targets. Buttons and links are at least 44 by 44 points.
  • Any orientation. The app works in portrait and landscape, including on iPad.
  • No time limits and no flashing content.

Known limits

  • The app uses a light appearance only, even when the phone is set to dark mode.
  • Very large text sizes can make the comparison of two backups long to scroll; the content stays readable.

How we test

Before each release we go through backup, restore, export and merge with VoiceOver on iPhone and TalkBack on Android, at default and at the largest text size, and check colour contrast against WCAG 2.2.

Tell us about a problem

If something in the app or on this website is hard to use with your setup, please write to us and say what you were trying to do and which phone and assistive technology you use. We aim to reply within 5 working days.
