PrintCal: Print Your Calendar

Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-30

The short version Nothing leaves your phone. PrintCal has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no internet access of its own. It reads your calendars on the phone to lay them out on a page, and never changes them. Its App Store privacy label and Google Play Data safety form both say: no data collected.

What the app reads

With your permission, the app reads the calendars on your phone: every calendar your Calendar app shows, such as iCloud, Google, Outlook or Exchange, subscribed, shared and local calendars, birthdays and holidays. On iPhone and iPad, with a separate permission, it can also read your Reminders lists (a Pro feature). It uses that access only to lay out your events on the page you asked for.

The reading happens on the phone. Events are kept in memory only while a page is built and are never written to storage or to logs. Access is read-only: PrintCal never adds, edits or deletes an event or a reminder. On Android, the app does not even request the permission that would allow writing.

What the app keeps

Only your choices: paper size, view, style, which calendars are ticked, your presets and the names you give them, the custom page title you type, how many times you have printed, the date you first opened the app, the date the app last asked you for a rating, which version of the Terms of use and of this policy you agreed to and when, and the code of the last error. These are stored in the app's settings file on the device only. Never an event's title, place, notes or link.

The PDF you print or share is written to a temporary folder on the phone that is emptied the next time the app starts. Print, Share and Save hand it only to the phone's print dialog or to the app you pick, such as Files, Google Drive or Mail; that app's own privacy terms then apply.

On iPhone and iPad, these settings are part of your device's own iCloud or computer backup, like any other app's data; they hold no events. On Android, the app opts out of Google device backup, so nothing of it is backed up.

What the app sends

Nothing. The app has no internet access of its own: on Android it blocks the internet permission entirely, and on iPhone it makes no network connections. It contains no analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting tools. If you have turned on crash or analytics sharing in your phone's settings, Apple or Google may receive reports through their own systems; those never include calendar data.

Links in Settings, such as this policy, open in your web browser. The browser, not the app, makes that connection.

Do Not Track

Neither the app nor this website tracks you over time or across other websites or apps, and neither lets any third party do so. Browser "Do Not Track" and Global Privacy Control signals are therefore treated the same way for everyone: there is no tracking to turn off. (California's online privacy law, CalOPPA, asks us to say this plainly.)

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play. The store takes the payment and keeps the receipt; the app only asks the store on the phone whether Pro is owned, so that it works on every device signed in to the same account or, on iPhone, the same Family Sharing group. We never see your payment details, and there is no ClientLens server involved.

When you email us

If you write to support, we receive what you send: your email address, your message and anything you attach. The app's Contact support button prepares an email with the app version, your phone's system and model, the number of calendars the app can see, the code of the last error and which version of the Terms of use and of this policy you agreed to. You see the whole email and can edit it before it is sent from your own mail app. It never includes an event.

We use these emails only to answer you and to fix problems you report. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering requests you send us. We keep support emails for up to 24 months after the conversation ends, then delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

Our email is handled by our email provider, which stores messages on our behalf.

Notifications

The app sends no notifications.

How long data is kept

Your settings stay on the phone until you change them or delete the app. Deleting the app removes them. PDFs in the temporary folder are removed at the next launch; copies you saved or shared elsewhere are under your control.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

The app collects no data, so for app use we hold nothing to show, correct or delete for you. Everything stays under your control, on your phone.

If you have emailed us, you can ask for a copy of those emails, for corrections, or for their deletion, and you can object to our use of them, by writing to the address below. We answer within 30 days. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live (in the EU or UK, your national regulator).

Users in India can raise a grievance with our grievance contact at the address below; we acknowledge it within 72 hours and resolve it within 30 days.
