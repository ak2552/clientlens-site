PrintCal: Print Your Calendar

Help and support

Most answers are below. If yours isn't, write to us and say which phone you have and what you were trying to print. Please don't send your calendar; we never need it.

support@clientlens.co

In the app, Settings → Contact support opens an email with your app version and phone model already filled in. You can read and edit it before sending.

Calendar access

Why can't the app see my calendar?

PrintCal reads calendars only with your permission, and the page stays blank until it has it. On iPhone, turn it on under Settings → Apps → PrintCal → Calendars → Full Access. On Android, Settings → Apps → PrintCal → Permissions → Calendar → Allow. Come back to the app and it re-checks on its own, no restart needed. Settings → Calendar access in the app shows what it can see right now.

What is "limited" or "add events only" access on iPhone?

iOS can give an app write-only access: the app may add events but cannot read any. Printing needs to read, so it needs full access. Open Settings → Apps → PrintCal → Calendars and choose Full Access.

Does the app change my events?

No. Access is used to read only; PrintCal never adds, edits or deletes an event, and never sends one anywhere.

A calendar I use is not in the list

PrintCal lists the calendars your phone's calendar app shows. On iPhone, a calendar that is off under Calendar → Calendars is hidden from every app; turn it on there and it appears in PrintCal. Subscribed and shared calendars show too, once iOS has downloaded them. On Android, a calendar you have unticked in Google Calendar or Samsung Calendar is hidden the same way; tick it there, and check Settings → Accounts → the account → Calendar sync is on.

The calendar is there but the month is empty

Open the Calendars sheet on the page and check that the calendar is ticked. Holidays switch off by themselves when a month has more than ten of them; turn them back on in the same sheet.

Pages, printers and paper

A day was full. Where did the rest of its events go?

On an overflow page right after the month, grouped by date. The day's cell shows "+N more" so you know to look there. Nothing is dropped. If you would rather have everything in the grid, choose a smaller text size, hide some details, or print the week instead.

Which printers work?

Any printer your phone can print to: AirPrint printers on iPhone and iPad, and any printer set up in Android's print service. Print opens the phone's own print dialog, so the choice of printer, copies and double-sided is made there. With no printer nearby, choose Save as PDF and print the file from a computer.

Letter or A4?

PrintCal picks the paper from your region: Letter in the US, Canada, Mexico and the Philippines, A4 everywhere else. Change it in Style on the page, or set a default under Settings → Defaults → Paper. The page is laid out for that size, so pick the same size in the print dialog.

The print is small or cut off

Check that the paper in the print dialog matches the page (Letter or A4) and that scaling is 100% or Fit. The page keeps its own margin, so borderless printing is not needed.

Where does the PDF go?

Tap Share and choose where: Files or iCloud Drive on iPhone, Google Drive or Downloads on Android, or any app that accepts a PDF, such as Mail. The file is named after what it holds, for example "Calendar 2027-01 Month.pdf". The app keeps the PDF only in a temporary folder that is emptied at the next launch, so save a copy where you want it.

Is the printed page in my language?

The app is in English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese (Brazil) and Japanese and follows your phone's language. Month and weekday names on the page follow it too. The page's own small labels, such as "All day" and "+N more", are in English in this release.

Free, Pro and purchases

What is free and what is Pro?

Free: month, week and list views, all your calendars and real events with saved selections, event details, text size, bold, Large Print and auto-fit, week start, weekdays only, day hours, Letter and A4, one month or one week at a time, one saved preset and a small footer line on the page. Pro, one purchase: day view and year view, any date range up to 24 months in one PDF, custom title, mini months and a notes column, four print fonts with separate sizes, custom calendar colours, Legal, Tabloid, A3, A5 and poster sizes, unlimited presets and no footer line; on iPhone also Reminders checklists, home-screen quick actions and Shortcuts; on Android, home-screen app shortcuts. The price is shown in your store. No subscription.

I bought Pro. How do I get it on a family member's iPhone?

Pro is one purchase on your App Store account, shared through Family Sharing. On the family member's device open Settings → Restore purchase in the app; the App Store finds the purchase through the family. If it says nothing was found, check iPhone Settings → Family → the buyer → Share Purchases with Family.

And on Android?

Pro belongs to the Google account that bought it and works on every device signed in to that account. Sign in with it, open Settings → Restore purchase in the app, and Pro turns on.

Restore says no purchase was found

Make sure the device is signed in to the account (or family) that bought Pro, then tap Restore purchase again. Purchases on the App Store and Google Play are separate: Pro bought on an iPhone does not unlock an Android phone, and the other way round.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple or Google, so they handle refunds. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open your order history in Google Play, or use the Google Play Help Center. If a refund is granted, Pro turns off the next time the app checks with the store.
