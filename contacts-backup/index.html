Nothing leaves your phone. No account, no server, no ads, no analytics. The app has no internet access at all. Your contacts go somewhere only when you send a file yourself.

What it does

Back up completelyAll accounts at once: iCloud, Gmail, Exchange. Phones, emails, addresses, birthdays, photos at full size, and a count of every field saved.
Restore with a previewSee what would come back before anything changes. Restore one contact or many. A fresh backup is always saved first.
Export to Excel or CSVPick your columns or use ready-made layouts for Google Contacts, Outlook and newsletter tools.
Merge duplicates safelyReview pairs side by side. Only clear matches are merged in bulk, and every merge can be undone.
History you can readEach backup shows what changed since the one before. Compare any two, or bring back a single contact.
Standard filesBackups are ordinary vCard files that any phone, Mac, PC or mail app can open.

Good to know

On iPhone, Apple only lets apps read contact notes with special permission, so notes are not included in backups on iPhone. Every other field is.
