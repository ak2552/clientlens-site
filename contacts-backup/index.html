Contacts Backup, Export, Merge

Back up every contact, every field, in one tap.

App Store · coming soon Google Play · coming soon

Contacts Backup, Export, Merge is an iPhone and Android app that saves a complete copy of your contacts as a standard vCard file, exports them to Excel or CSV, and cleans up duplicate contacts safely. Use it before switching phones, after a sync mistake, or whenever you want a copy of your address book that you control.

We never see your contacts. No account, no server, no ads, no analytics. The app has no internet access at all. Backups are kept in the app on your phone, and are included in your phone's own iCloud or Google backup if you use one.

What it does

Back up all your contactsEvery account at once: iCloud, Gmail, Exchange. Phone numbers, emails, addresses, birthdays, full-size photos, and a count of every field saved.
Restore with a previewSee what would come back before anything changes. Restore one contact or many. A fresh backup is always saved first.
Export contacts to Excel or CSVPick your columns or use ready-made layouts for Google Contacts, Outlook and Mailchimp-style newsletter tools. JSON too.
Merge duplicate contacts safelyReview pairs side by side. Only clear matches are merged in bulk, and every merge can be undone.
Backup history you can readEach backup shows what changed since the one before. Compare any two, or bring back a single deleted contact.
Standard vCard filesBackups are ordinary .vcf files that any phone, Mac, PC, Gmail or Outlook can import.

How to back up your iPhone contacts

  1. Open the app and allow access to contacts.
  2. Tap Back Up Now.
  3. Choose where to keep the file: in the app, Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, AirDrop or Mail.
  4. Check the report. It counts every phone number, email and photo that was saved, so you know nothing was dropped.

How to export contacts to Excel or CSV

  1. Open the Export tab and pick Excel or CSV.
  2. Choose a layout, such as Google Contacts or Outlook, or pick your own columns.
  3. Check the preview, then tap Export and send the file where you need it.

How to merge duplicate contacts

  1. Open the Duplicates tab and tap Scan.
  2. Review each group side by side and pick the card to keep. Details from the other card are added to it.
  3. Tap Merge. Changed your mind? Tap Undo all, or restore the backup saved before the merge.
