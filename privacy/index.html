This website

Last updated: 30 October 2026

The short version clientlens.co is a plain, static website. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from third parties. The only record of your visit is the standard server log kept by our host, GitHub Pages.

What this website does not do

  • No cookies. The site stores nothing in your browser.
  • No analytics. We do not count visitors, measure clicks or build profiles.
  • No third-party scripts, fonts or embeds. Every page is made of our own HTML and CSS, using the fonts already on your device. Your browser contacts no other company to show it.
  • No forms and no accounts. The only way to reach us is by email, which you send from your own mail app.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub keeps standard server logs of visits, which include your IP address and browser type, to keep the service secure and running. GitHub holds these logs under its own policy, not ours; see GitHub's privacy statement and its note on GitHub Pages data collection. We do not receive or look at these logs.

Do Not Track

This website does not track you over time or across other websites, and lets no third party do so. Browser "Do Not Track" and Global Privacy Control signals are therefore treated the same way for everyone: there is no tracking to turn off.

When you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach, and we use them only to answer you. How long we keep support emails and what rights you have over them are set out in each app's own privacy policy, which is linked from that app's page.

The apps

This note covers the website only. Each ClientLens app has its own privacy policy, which describes what that app does with your data. You will find it on the app's page on this site, inside the app, and on its App Store and Google Play listing.

Changes

If this note changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
